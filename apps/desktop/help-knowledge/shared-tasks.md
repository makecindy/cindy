---
id: shared-tasks
title: Sharing a task with other people
summary: Share a task with other Cindy accounts through an invitation link (Share Task in the task menu); guests see the full history and message the same Agent. Manage sharing in Settings > Share Task; to send a copy instead, export a .cshare file.
---
**Share Task** lets people on **other Cindy accounts** join one of your tasks. They see its full history, tool results, and attachments, and can message the same Agent. The task keeps running on your computer (the "host"), using your models and connections. To use your own phone or other computers on the **same account**, you don't need sharing; see the Remote topic.

**Starting to share (host):**

- Open the task's menu (the **⋯** next to the task title, or right-click it in the sidebar) and choose **Share Task**, then **Start Sharing**. You must be signed in, and the task must be active (not archived). Tasks running on your other computers can be shared too.
- **Copy invitation** copies a message with the invitation link. Send it to your teammate. Anyone holding the link can join after signing in, with the same Agent access as you, and without needing your approval.
- A task can have up to **2 guests**. There's also a limit on how many tasks you can host or join at once.
- Sharing ends automatically if the host computer stays offline. To resume, start sharing again and send a new invitation link.

**Managing a shared task (host):**

- In the task menu, **Manage Sharing** has **Copy invitation**, **Manage Members…** (see members and **Remove** a guest), and **Stop Sharing**.
- Removing a guest or stopping sharing cuts off access right away and makes old invitation links invalid. The task history and the Agent's work are kept.
- Stop sharing before you archive or delete a shared task.

**Joining (guest):**

- Open the invitation link: it opens Cindy and joins you right away, then you **Enter Task**. If Cindy doesn't open, choose **Join Shared Task** and paste the link (an older invitation code also works).
- Where to find **Join Shared Task**: the **+** next to the **Share Task** group in the left sidebar (the group appears once you host or join something), or **Settings > Share Task**.
- As a guest you can send messages, add attachments, use voice input, stop the Agent, and answer the Agent's permission prompts, questions, and plan reviews. The task's Agent, model, and reasoning settings can only be changed by the host. Side chats and forking aren't available to guests.
- The task's menu only shows **Leave** for guests. To rejoin later, you need a new invitation link from the host.
- An invitation only works in a Cindy app that uses the same service — for example, an invitation from the Mainland China edition can't be used in the other edition.

**Settings > Share Task:**

- **Shared by Me**: every task you share (including from your other computers) — click a title to open it, **Manage** to see members and the invitation, or **Stop All Sharing**.
- **Joined by Me**: tasks you joined — click a title to open it, or **Leave**.
- **Join Shared Task** button: paste an invitation link or code.

**In the sidebar:**

- Shared tasks you host or joined appear together in the **Share Task** group. Tasks you host show a gold crown.

**Sending a copy instead of live sharing:**

- To give someone a snapshot of a task, use **Export session…** in the task menu to create a `.cshare` file (optionally password-protected). The other person imports it on their own computer and continues independently. See the Importing sessions topic.

**Notes:**

- Only invite people you trust: guests use the Agent with the task's existing permissions, tools, and plugins, and can read and change files in the task's working folder.
- Ending sharing doesn't take back anything guests already saw or downloaded.
